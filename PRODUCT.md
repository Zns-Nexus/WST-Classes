# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Students in Classes 10 to 12 around Naya Nagar, Mira Road, choosing coaching for SSC, HSC, NEET, JEE, MHT-CET or HSC Computer Science. They judge a class by its teachers, its energy and whether it feels like a place where they will understand things.
- Parents, who usually make the final decision and pay fees. They need credibility: results, structure, location and an easy way to ask about batches and fees.

The site must speak to students first, show results, and convince parents.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for Work Smart Tutorials, a coaching institute. Success means a visitor finds the right course quickly, gets a feel for the teaching, and starts an enquiry.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood coaching class in Naya Nagar with its own teachers on YouTube (Work Smart SSC and Work Smart HSC channels). Tagline in use: "Don't just work hard. Work smart."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Effect. pnpm only.
- Courses (confirmed): SSC, HSC, NEET, JEE, MHT-CET, HSC Computer Science.
- Enquiries link to the official Instagram accounts. No form storage or submission API.
- Student portal is not connected. It must say so and must not accept credentials.
- Search indexing stays disabled until the institute approves launch.
- Pinned layout brief: full-size header and a rotating photographic hero carousel. Keep these, and improve on them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Work Smart Tutorials. The WST logo (blue W and T, maroon S, black outline) is taken from the official HSC YouTube avatar until the owner supplies an original file.
- Brand colours: blue `#3f3c9b`, maroon `#7e2634`, navy `#213f73`, cream `#d9cfbe` (from the logo and HSC channel banner).
- Style direction confirmed by the user: professional, in line with large Indian education institutes (ALLEN, Aakash, Physics Wallah). Rejected: navy/teal, and a dark condensed-type poster style.
- The user approved using real teacher imagery (YouTube lesson thumbnails) as the main visual proof, with stock photos secondary and labelled as illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Real teachers and real lessons are the strongest proof; show them before stock imagery.
2. Results matter to parents, so the results area must be ready to showcase them, but it stays honest until verified numbers arrive.
3. Every page leads to one next step: find a course, then enquire.
4. Speak like a local teacher, not a national chain.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many visitors are on phones. Keep text readable at small sizes, touch targets at least 44px, keyboard-usable carousel and menus, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
